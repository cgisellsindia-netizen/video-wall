color images even in extremely low-light or nighttime conditions. Unlike traditional infrared (IR) cameras 
 
that produce black and-white footage in the dark, Full-Color Night Vision cameras use advanced sensors 
 
and supplemental lighting to deliver clear and colorful video.

AI-Powered Smart Detection is an advanced feature in modern CCTV cameras that uses artiﬁcial 
intelligence algorithms to analyze video footage in real-time. Instead of just recording footage, these 
cameras intelligently identify and diﬀerentiate between people, vehicles, and other objects, providing 
precise and actionable alerts.
Remote Access via Mobile App
Remote Access via Mobile App allows users to monitor and manage their CCTV camera systems directly 
from their smartphones or tablets, oﬀering real-time surveillance and control from anywhere in the world. 
With a user-friendly interface, it ensures round-the-clock security and convenience at your ﬁngertips.
